Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

Will this work for me?

It works best if you are honest about your situation and ready to act on the guidance. The program is built for students unsure about their field, people stuck in the wrong job, career switchers and overthinkers. There is no magic answer, but you leave with a clear direction and a written plan. Not sure? Start with the 5 Min Call (PKR 1,000), where we do a suitability check first.

What are your qualifications?

ARC is pursuing a BS in Psychology through Virtual University and has completed courses in Shariah and Tafseer of the Quran. He has 5+ years of experience and has guided 500+ clients across 10+ countries. This is career mentorship and counseling, not medical or clinical treatment.

Is the program available for females?

Yes, females are welcome. Sessions are held on WhatsApp voice call. The physical meeting can be held if her parents or a mahram come along with her (a parent or sibling).

What is the Career Mentorship Program?

A step-by-step guidance program to help you find direction, make better decisions and build a meaningful future. It includes 2 online sessions, 1 physical session, a SWOT analysis with a written report, and 3 months of support.

What if I want to leave the program halfway?

If you leave before completing the program, 30% of your payment is deducted and the remaining 70% is refunded to you.

What is your refund policy?

The Complete Program comes with a 100% money-back guarantee. Complete the full program: all your sessions, the physical meeting, the SWOT analysis and your 120 minutes of support. If you still want a refund after that, send your request within 7 days of completing the program and you will get your full 100% payment back. If you leave before completing, 30% is deducted and 70% is refunded. Refunds are returned 7 days after your request. Every refund request must include a short written reason (a few lines on what did not work for you). The 5 Min Call payment is non-refundable.

How do I reschedule a session?

Just let us know at least 24 hours before your session date and we will reschedule it free of cost.

How does the 3 months of support work?

You get a total credit of 120 minutes of support. It can be used on voice chat or text chat, and usually happens on WhatsApp audio call. You can use it in any way that suits you within the 3 months: all in one day, over a week, across a month, or spread over the full 3 months.

How are sessions held?

The online sessions are held over WhatsApp, Zoom or Google Meet. The physical meeting is held in Lahore, Islamabad or Sargodha, whichever is nearest to you. Mention your city in the form and we will confirm the exact place with you. Females are welcome: sessions run on WhatsApp voice call, and the physical meeting can be held with her parents or a mahram.

What does it cost and how do I pay?

The 5 Min Call is PKR 1,000 and the Complete Program is PKR 50,000. Pay by EasyPaisa or bank transfer, then send your receipt on WhatsApp.

How many seats are available?

To give every client proper attention, there are only 12 seats per month. Apply early to secure your seat.

How soon will I hear back?

You’ll receive confirmation within 24 hours, and our team will contact you on WhatsApp.

How does the SWOT analysis work?

SWOT is a simple but powerful way to see where you really stand. After your sessions, you receive a personalised written report that looks at four things: your Strengths (what you are naturally good at), your Weaknesses (the gaps and habits holding you back), the Opportunities (the paths and options open to you) and the Threats (the risks and obstacles to watch out for). Together, it gives you an honest, clear picture of your situation and a direction for your next steps.

Is this therapy?

No. This is career mentorship and counseling, not medical treatment or psychotherapy. If you are dealing with a clinical mental-health condition, we will advise you to also consult a licensed professional.

Is my information confidential?

Yes. Your details and what you share stay private, and sessions are not recorded without your permission.
